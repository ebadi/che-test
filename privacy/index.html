Privacy Policy

The privacy and protection of your data is of great concern to us. This document describes any collection, usage and disclosure of personal information when using Chedoku's website and apps. It also explains your rights and how they are protected.

We collect and process your data exclusively based on existing laws — e.g. the European General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"). Collected personal information is used only to provide and improve our services; we never use or share it except as described in this policy. By using our services, you agree to this privacy policy.

Your Data Protection Rights

Under the GDPR, you have:

  • Right of access to the personal information we gather about you.
  • Right to correction of your personal information.
  • Right to erasure of your personal data from our servers.
  • Right to complain to a supervisory authority about our handling of your data.
  • Right to restrict processing of your personal information.
  • Right to object to how your personal information is processed.
  • Right to portability of your personal information.

Data Controller

Data Security

All communication between your device and our services is encrypted via SSL — you can verify this in your browser via the lock icon in the address bar. Data is stored on our processors' servers under contracts compliant with Article 28 of the GDPR.

Cookies

Our website and apps use small text files ("cookies") stored on your device to enable certain functionality. On your first visit, only technically mandatory, first-party cookies are used.

First-party cookies

session — stores information about your current visit (e.g. login state). Written on every visit, expires at the end of the session.

Third-party cookies

_ga, _gat, _gid — written by Google Analytics, used to understand and improve the site. Set only if you accept the analytics cookie notice.

Data Collection, Processing and Usage

Webhosting

Visiting our site transmits standard technical data (IP address, visit time/duration, pages visited, browser/OS type) to our hosting provider, GitHub, Inc. This is required for the site to function and is never used to identify or track you personally. Processed under Article 6(1) of the GDPR.

Web Analytics

If you accept the analytics cookie notice, anonymized usage data is forwarded to Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited) to help us understand and improve the site. IP addresses are pseudonymized before storage, retained for 26 months. Processed under Article 6(1a) of the GDPR (opt-in only).

E-Mail Submission

Our mailing-list form collects your name and e-mail address and transmits them via our processor, Sendinblue Inc., to add you to our list.

Outgoing Links

Our website and apps link to other websites we don't operate. We have no control over their content or privacy practices — please check their own policies.
